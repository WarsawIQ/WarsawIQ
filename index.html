What we do

Three services. Each is backed by work done inside regulated businesses, not advised on from outside.

Data governance and compliance

Certification programmes, policy frameworks, and the evidence trail that holds up when an external auditor pulls on it.

  • Gap assessment against ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with a costed remediation plan
  • Policy framework and technical and organisational measures
  • Audit preparation: evidence packs, control narratives, dry runs
  • Outsourced DPO under the GDPR, or DPA under the Swiss FADP

Talks and training

Security awareness people actually remember, and technical training that leaves a certification behind.

  • Security awareness for mixed technical and non-technical audiences
  • AI literacy for leadership teams
  • Cloud and machine-learning courses with a certification path
  • Conference keynotes and invited lectures

Applied research and development

Machine learning on difficult signals — speech, gaze, language, network data — built to be reproduced, not demonstrated once.

  • Feasibility studies and proofs of concept with an honest verdict
  • ML pipelines that run on-premise or in a public cloud
  • Data quality, validation and enrichment
  • Joint research and grant collaborations with universities
